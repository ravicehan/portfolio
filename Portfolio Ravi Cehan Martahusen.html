Executive Summary

About Me

I am a law graduate from Universitas Diponegoro (Graduated Cum Laude with a 3.65 GPA) and a Supreme Court-certified mediator (C.Med.), currently serving as a Junior Associate at Raden Elang Mulyana Law Office.

My professional focus centers on five key practice areas: Criminal Defense, Civil & Administrative Law, Employment & Labor Law, Commercial Law, and Mediation & Dispute Resolution.

Prior to joining the firm, I accumulated intensive hands-on legal experience through internships at the Attorney General’s Office of Indonesia (Kejaksaan Agung RI - Pidsus Unit), working on landmark high-profile corruption, bribery, and state asset recovery cases, as well as holding leadership roles in student government and procedural law organizations.
